Learn Blockchain with Phoebe

Harbourgate is a port authority keeping a custody register: who signed for which consignment, who inspected it, and whether the seal was intact. Change one entry in that register and a check fires immediately. Recompute the hash to cover your tracks and the failure does not disappear, it moves one block along. Hide it completely and you have to redo every block that follows. That is the whole mechanism, and it is a genuinely clever one. It is also not magic: a chain preserves what was written and has no opinion whatever on whether it was true. Six leader sessions on the mechanism and its limits, ten practitioner sessions building one, and a real SHA-256 that ships its published test vectors because a hash that is subtly wrong still produces plausible looking hex.
